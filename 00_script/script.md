# 🎬 "THE UNREAD LETTER" — Short Film Script (~4 min)

**Genre:** Emotional drama
**Main character:** AYAN (you) — *rename to anything you like*
**Theme:** A son, too busy and distant, loses his father — and finds the letter he never read.
**Locations (4):** Apartment (night) · Childhood home (day) · Park bench (golden hour) · Cemetery (dawn)

> Design notes for AI: ONE adult face throughout (your LoRA) — no child/young flashbacks (AI can't
> de-age you consistently). Memories are shown through the empty home + objects, not a younger you.
> Lots of close-ups (AI's strength), simple slow camera, few locations = strong consistency.
> The father is shown only by hands, his coat, a photo, the back of his head — so we don't need a
> second consistent face. This keeps it 100% doable.

---

## SCENE 1 — INT. AYAN'S APARTMENT — NIGHT
*Cold, blue light. A modern, lonely apartment. AYAN (30s, tired, grey sweater) opens a cardboard
box of his late father's belongings. An old coat, a watch, photos. He pulls out a sealed, yellowed
envelope. His name is on it, in his father's handwriting.*

**AYAN** *(quiet, to himself)*
> "You never said you wrote me anything."

*He holds the letter but can't open it. He sets it down. Looks at an old photo — him and his father,
years ago.*

**AYAN** *(whisper)*
> "I was always too busy, Baba. Always next week."

---

## SCENE 2 — INT. CHILDHOOD HOME — DAY (MEMORY)
*Warm golden light. An empty old family home, dust in the sunbeams. AYAN walks through it slowly,
touching the walls. We see his father's empty armchair, his reading glasses on a table, a half-finished
cup of tea. The father is felt everywhere but never shown fully.*

**AYAN** *(softly)*
> "It still smells like you in here."

*He sits in the empty armchair. Closes his eyes.*

**AYAN**
> "I'm sorry I stopped coming."

---

## SCENE 3 — EXT. PARK BENCH — GOLDEN HOUR
*A quiet park, warm low sun. The bench where his father used to sit and feed the birds. AYAN sits
alone. He finally takes out the letter. His hands tremble. He opens it.*

**FATHER (V.O.)** *(warm, aged voice — your cloned voice, lower/older tone)*
> "My son. If you are reading this, I am already gone. Do not be sad for too long."

*AYAN's eyes fill. A single tear.*

**FATHER (V.O.)**
> "I was never angry that you were busy. I was proud. You were building your life. That was all I
> ever wanted."

---

## SCENE 4 — EXT. CEMETERY — DAWN
*Soft pink-orange dawn light, mist. AYAN stands at his father's grave, the open letter in his hand.
He finally speaks — calm, at peace.*

**FATHER (V.O.)** *(gentle)*
> "Live well. Laugh loudly. And come sit on the bench sometimes. I'll be there."

**AYAN** *(a small, broken smile)*
> "I will, Baba. Every Sunday. I promise."

*He places a flower on the grave. The sun rises fully. He walks away — lighter. The camera holds on
the empty bench from Scene 3, now with a single bird landing on it.*

**FADE TO BLACK.**

> **TITLE CARD:** *"For the ones we forgot to thank."*
