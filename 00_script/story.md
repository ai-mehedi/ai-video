# Story — "The Last Rickshaw" (test film, ~36 sec)

**Logline:** A poor old rickshaw puller in Old Dhaka quietly sacrifices everything to pay for
his daughter's school admission.

**Main character:** KARIM — 60s, thin, weathered, kind tired eyes, grey beard.
**Tone:** Emotional drama. Rain, neon, Old Dhaka.
**Why this test:** weathered face (AI does well), rain hides flaws, only 2 locations, short — a clean
way to test the WHOLE pipeline (image → video → voice → music) and judge video quality.

**Beats:**
1. Karim pulls his empty rickshaw through a rainy neon alley at dawn — tired but moving.
2. He looks at a wet photo of his daughter in school uniform. *"Aar ektu, ma. Aar ektu."*
3. He adds the day's coins to a small tin box.
4. He pulls harder through a busy rainy street.
5. At the school gate he counts wet money — just enough.
6. Outside, he looks up and smiles, rain hiding his tears. *"Hoye gechhe."* ("It's done.")

**Title card:** *"For every father who gave everything."*
