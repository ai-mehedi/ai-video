# 🎬 Google Flow Guide — make your film the EASY way (GUI, no command line)

Google Flow (built on Veo 3.1) = browser app, premium quality, audio included, character
consistency via "Ingredients". Best path if the DIY pipeline is too hard.

## 1. Setup (one time)
- Go to **labs.google/flow** → sign in with Google.
- Subscribe to **Google AI Pro ($19.99/mo)** = ~50 Veo Fast clips/month = a short film.
  (🎓 students: free for 1 year.)

## 2. Make your reusable character (like Soul ID / LoRA, but easy)
- Use **"Ingredients to Video"** → upload up to **3 reference images** of Karim.
- Get those references from your FREE FLUX keyframe (or generate once in Flow).
- Flow keeps the SAME character across every shot. This is your consistency fix. ✅

## 3. The workflow per shot
1. New scene → pick **Ingredients** (your Karim reference)
2. Paste the shot prompt (below)
3. Generate → Veo makes **video + sound** together
4. Use **camera controls** (type the move) + **scene extension** if you need longer
5. Download the clip

## 4. Finish
- Download all clips → drop in **DaVinci Resolve** (free) → trim, color grade, grain → export.
- Veo already gives audio, so less work than the DIY pipeline.

# 🎥 READY-TO-PASTE PROMPTS — "The Last Rickshaw"

> Veo likes rich, descriptive prompts with camera + mood + sound cues. Paste one per shot.

**Shot 01 — wide, alley**
> Cinematic wide shot, slow tracking forward. A weathered 60-year-old Bangladeshi rickshaw puller
> with grey beard and tired kind eyes pulls his empty rickshaw through a narrow Old Dhaka alley at
> dawn. Heavy monsoon rain, neon shop signs reflecting on wet ground. Moody, film grain, 35mm.
> Sound: heavy rain, distant city hum.

**Shot 02 — close-up, face**
> Cinematic close-up, slow push in. Rain drips from the old rickshaw puller's grey hair and beard,
> his tired determined eyes looking ahead. Old Dhaka, dawn, neon glow, realistic wet skin texture.
> Sound: soft rain on skin, gentle ambience.

**Shot 03 — medium, photo (dialogue)**
> Medium shot, static. Under a shop awning, the old rickshaw puller takes a folded wet photo of his
> young daughter in school uniform from his chest pocket and looks at it tenderly. Warm shop light,
> rain behind. He whispers in Bangla: "Aar ektu, ma. Aar ektu." Sound: rain, faint shop sounds.

**Shot 04 — close-up, hands**
> Close-up, static. The old man's weathered hands open a small rusty tin box full of coins and add
> a few wet coins. Warm light, rain. Sound: coins clinking, rain.

**Shot 05 — wide, busy street**
> Cinematic wide, handheld follow. The rickshaw puller pulls harder through a busy rainy Old Dhaka
> street, headlights and traffic, splashing water, determined face. Sound: rickshaw bell, traffic,
> heavy rain.

**Shot 06 — medium, school gate**
> Medium shot, static. At a school admission desk, the old man counts wet crumpled money with
> trembling hands — just enough. Soft daylight, rain outside, emotional. Sound: paper, distant rain.

**Shot 07 — close-up, ending (dialogue)**
> Cinematic close-up, slow push in. Outside the school gate in the rain, the old rickshaw puller
> looks up at the building and smiles — proud, tired, rain hiding his tears. Soft light. He whispers
> in Bangla: "Hoye gechhe." Sound: gentle rain, emotional silence.

## Tips for Veo/Flow
- Keep character words consistent every shot (same age/beard/eyes) + use the Ingredient image.
- Add camera move + lighting + a "Sound:" line — Veo uses them.
- If a face looks off, regenerate (you have monthly credits) or use First/Last frame control.
- Veo clips are ~8 sec — perfect for these shots.
